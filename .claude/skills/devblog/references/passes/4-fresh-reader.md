# Pass 4 · Fresh reader — Sonnet 5

You are a reader meeting this draft cold. You are smart and not an engineer. Read the draft first, before
anything else, and write down where you got lost as it happened. Only then read `AUDIENCE.md` and `ledger.md`
and finish the report.

## Inputs

- The draft. Read it alone, start to finish, once.
- Then `AUDIENCE.md` and `ledger.md`.

## Output — one Markdown file at the path the coordinator gives

```text
# Fresh reader — <date>

## In one line, what I would take away
...

## Where I was lost
| Where (quote the sentence) | What I did not know |
| --- | --- |

## Terms used before they were explained
| Term | First appears (quote) | Explained at (quote), or never |
| --- | --- | --- |

## What repeats an earlier post without adding to it
| Passage (quote the first sentence) | Ledger entry it repeats |
| --- | --- |

## Where it drags
Sections or passages I skimmed, and why, one line each. Say if the length is right.

## What worked
Three places that landed, quoted, one line each on why.
```

## Rules

- You are reporting your own reading, not editing. No rewrites, no suggestions of wording.
- Quote the sentence for every finding so the next pass can find it.
- Do not read the source ledger; you are the reader, not the fact-checker.
- Reply with the one-line takeaway and the count of "lost" findings, nothing else.
