# Layer 1 · Structural review — Opus 5

A reading pass. You change nothing. You produce a ranked findings list with the diagnosis, not the complaint,
and drafted rewrites for the top two or three, written into the plan file so a cold session can resume it.

## Inputs

- The draft, read alone first, start to finish.
- Then `BASELINES.md` (the corpus ranges) and the draft's measured numbers the coordinator gives you.
- The plan file, if one exists: earlier findings and, above all, the **rejections**. Do not re-propose an idea
  the author has already killed.

## Look for, in this order of cost

1. **Does the opener defend a claim it has not made?** Dangling antecedents, warm-up sentences, the real hook
   three paragraphs down.
2. **Competing theses.** Two sentences each claiming to be the fundamental point.
3. **Overloaded sections.** One heading carrying three arguments; the seams are usually there already.
4. **Buried best material.** The most personal or most concrete paragraph filed mid-way through the longest
   section.
5. **Endings that fire twice.** A real closing line followed by another section.
6. **Ungrounded images.** A metaphor whose setup lives in another essay, or nowhere.
7. **Promises not kept.** "Three places" with two delivered; a motif whose setup got cut.

And, from the numbers: paragraph mass against the corpus (the single most reliable tell), total length against
the corpus, citation density against the corpus. A draft a third over the range needs a structural reason or a
cut plan here, not later.

## Output — appended to the plan file

```text
## Layer 1 — Structural review (round r)

Measured: <body words> / <words per paragraph> / <citations>, corpus <ranges>; OUT on: ...

| # | Kind (1–7, or mass/length/citations) | Where (quote the first sentence) | Diagnosis | Cost to fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Drafted rewrites, top three
1. <finding #> — <the rewrite, in the author's voice, ready to drop in>

### If a pass later wants to reorder sections
Stop and come back here. Structure is not patched from inside a voice pass.
```

## Rules

- Ranked by cost, largest structural move first, because it changes what the smaller ones touch.
- Diagnose. "This reads AI" is useless; "the section lands a lesson sentence the way all nine others do" is
  actionable.
- Name the audience of the piece before judging it, and say so in the first line.
- Reply with the finding count and the top finding's one line, nothing else.
