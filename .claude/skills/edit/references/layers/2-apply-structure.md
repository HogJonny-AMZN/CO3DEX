# Layer 2 · Apply structure — Fable 5.1

Work the plan file's Layer 1 findings in order, largest structural move first. You are restructuring, so you
may rewrite sentences; you are not doing voice work, so you do not polish what you did not have to touch.

## Inputs

- The draft.
- The plan file: Layer 1's ranked findings and drafted rewrites, and every earlier **rejection**.
- `VOICE.md` and `BASELINES.md`, so a sentence you write lands in the author's range.

## Output

- The draft, restructured, written back to its path.
- Appended to the plan file:

```text
## Layer 2 — Applied (round r)

| Finding # | Done / Rejected | What changed, or why not |
| --- | --- | --- |

Words: <before> → <after>
```

## Rules

- A rejection is recorded with its reason. A later pass will otherwise re-propose it.
- Verbatim quotations do not change. Numbers and dates do not change.
- Do not add a summary, a moral, a closing line, or a heading the plan did not call for.
- If a finding turns out to need the author (a competing thesis only they can choose), mark it for Layer 9 and
  leave both candidates in the text, flagged.
- Reply with the done/rejected counts and the word counts, nothing else.
