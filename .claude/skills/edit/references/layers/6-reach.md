# Layer 6 · Reach — Opus 5 (optional; off by default)

Only for posts meant to travel. Skip for personal essays that are their own reward. The coordinator runs this
layer only when asked (`--reach`).

## Inputs

- The draft. You do not change its body.
- The front matter's `title` and `summary`, and the closer.
- The plan file.

## What to do

Invoke `contagious`, `made-to-stick` and `storybrand-messaging` with the Skill tool, on the title, the summary
and the closer only. The two levers that usually matter:

- **Triggers.** What recurring daily moment fires this post back into a reader's head? Frequency beats strength.
- **Practical value.** Is there a portable test the reader can apply elsewhere? If the heuristic exists but
  appears once mid-essay, restate it in the closer, in the author's words, once.

Title and `summary:` are the cheapest high-leverage changes. Summaries here make a claim and open a gap; they
do not describe the post. Read the published ones before writing a new one.

## Output

- Proposed title and summary alternatives (two or three each) and, if warranted, one closer sentence, written
  into the plan file for Layer 9. **The draft body is not edited by this layer.**

```text
## Layer 6 — Reach (round r)

| Skill | Score | What would raise it |
| --- | --- | --- |

### Title candidates
### Summary candidates
### Closer, if the heuristic needs restating
```

## Rules

- No body edits. No lesson sentence added to a section. This is a personal blog, not a landing page.
- Reply with the three scores, nothing else.
