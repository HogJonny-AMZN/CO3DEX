# Layer 9 · Author review — the owner

The author reads once, here, with a finished draft and a scorecard that says what moved and what did not. This
layer is not a subagent. The coordinator prepares the handover and stops.

## The coordinator writes, at the top of the plan file

```text
## For the author

**Thesis:** <one line, as the draft now states it>
**Deliberate trades:** <dimensions flat across three passes, and why they were left>
**Decisions waiting on you:** <competing theses left flagged in the text; claims marked [unverified]; footnotes
proposed for dropping as asserted-from-standing; title and summary candidates from Layer 6>
**Sentences a pass introduced rather than edited:** <quoted, with the layer that wrote each>
**Not worth your time:** punctuation fixes; everything in the Rejected tables.

Scorecard history: <round 1 → round n totals>. Length: <words by round>. Gates: <in/OUT each>.
```

Then the author reads the draft. Publishing mechanics (Layer 10 in `EDITORIAL_PIPELINE.md`) are the author's
checklist and are not run by the skill.
